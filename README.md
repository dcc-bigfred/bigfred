# BigFred

BigFred turns your phone or tablet into a full-featured DCC throttle over your local network. Drive locomotives, manage your fleet, and coordinate with other operators — no app store required.

Open the app in any browser, pick a locomotive, and drive. BigFred works with Roco/Fleischmann Z21, LocoNet, and WiThrottle command stations, and lets multiple operators share the same layout at once.

## What you get

- **Web throttle** — speed, direction, functions, and emergency stop from phone, tablet, or desktop
- **Roster & consists** — register vehicles and drive multi-loco trains as one unit
- **Leases** — lend control to another operator with time and speed limits
- **Interlockings** — coordinate busy junctions with radio chat and a signalman role
- **Remote handsets** — pair Z21 handsets and WiThrottle apps to vehicles in your roster
